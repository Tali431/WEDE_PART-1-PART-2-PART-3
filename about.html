About Darie Mae Robinson YPD

Grow, Glow and Go for Christ

Who Are We?🤷‍♀️

The D.M. Robinson Chapel A.M.E. Church Young People's Division (DMR YPD) is a Christian youth organisation dedicated to nurturing young people in their faith, strengthening their relationship with God, and equipping them to become responsible leaders in the church and community. We provide a welcoming space where young people can grow spiritually, discover their talents, build meaningful friendships, and learn the importance of serving others. Through worship, fellowship, youth programmes, community outreach, and various church activities, we encourage our members to live according to Christian values and become positive examples to those around them. As the YPD, we believe that young people are not only the future of the church but also an important part of its present. Our mission is to inspire, empower, and unite young people as we grow together in faith, leadership, and service, using our gifts and abilities to make a meaningful difference wherever God leads us.

Our History📜

The YPD has a long history within the AME Church. The AME Church was formally established in 1816 under the leadership of Richard Allen and it grew into a global church with a strong commitment to faith, education, leadership and service.The YPD was formally adopted within the missionary work of the AME in the early twentieth century developing programmes that gave young people opportunities to learn, serve, lead and become more involved in church life. Its purpose continues to include youth training, Christian education, leadership development and mission work.DMR YPD carries the legacy forward by creating opportunities for young people to connect, worship, learn, serve and grow together. We believe that young people are not just the future. We are an important part of the church today.
