Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the YPD?

Just come through during the Sunday Service at 09:00 AM! Anyone from 6 to 26 years is welcome.

Do I need to be a member of Darie Mae Robinson Church?

Yes, you need to be a church member first to join the DMR YPD, because we are a youth ministry of the church.

What is the age limit to join the YPD?

The age limit is 26. However there are other auxiliaries for different age groups, such as Young Adults.

What happens at YPD Meetings?

At YPD meetings, we have fellowship time, worship, and discussions about the organisation and duties.

How much do camps and retreats cost?

Cost varies by location and duration. We announce costs on the Events page and WhatsApp group.
